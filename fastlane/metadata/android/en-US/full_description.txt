Cabàs is a free and open personal finance app inspired by the Japanese Kakebo method.

You record income and expenses by hand, mindfully, and every month you see where your money goes.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Income and expenses with date, description and category, with suggestions from the descriptions you have used.</li>
<li>Editable Kakebo categories (needs, wants, culture and unexpected), each with an optional monthly budget.</li>
<li>Monthly summary with balance and expenses by category.</li>
<li>A spending budget and savings goal for every month.</li>
<li>Recurring transactions: rent, salary or subscriptions are recorded automatically.</li>
<li>End-of-month reflection with the four Kakebo questions.</li>
<li>Search across all months and a yearly trends chart.</li>
<li>Manual or weekly automatic backups, restore and CSV export.</li>
<li>Lock with your phone’s fingerprint or PIN and an optional daily reminder.</li>
<li>Light, dark or system theme. In Valencian, Spanish and English.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
Cabàs does not request Internet access. No ads, telemetry, accounts or bank connections. Your data stays on your device and you decide where your backups go.
